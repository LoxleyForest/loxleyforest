A January weekend, on us

Two nights in the trees. Free to enter.

One winner spends a Friday and Saturday night in the treehouse this January, with the Dawn Table carried across the bridge on the first morning. Nothing to buy, follow, tag or share. One entry each, by the form below.

The prize

Two nights in the treehouse, Friday and Saturday, on the weekend of January 8, 15, 22 or 29, 2027. The winner chooses from those still open when they claim the prize. If all four are booked, any open Friday and Saturday through March 2027.

The Dawn Table on your first morning: champagne brunch carried across the bridge to your door. Champagne is for guests 21 or older. And pizza night, as on every stay: fresh dough waiting in the treehouse.

Approximate retail value: $1,565, the dearest of the four weekends at our rates on November 7, 2026, with the Dawn Table. Odds depend on the number of entries. The winner pays nothing to claim or take the stay. The prize has no cash value, cannot be exchanged or transferred, and is for the winner's own stay, booked with us under our usual booking agreement. Any income tax on the prize is the winner's.

How to enter

Fill in the form below. That is the whole of it. No purchase, no follow, no tag, no share, and nothing you buy improves your chances.

One entry per person. A second entry from the same email address does not count. A plus-tag in any address, or dots in a Gmail address, do not make it a different address. Entries after the close do not count, nor does an entry without the eligibility box ticked.

Entries open on Thursday, November 12, 2026, at 9 AM Eastern Time and close on Sunday, December 13, 2026, at 11:59 PM Eastern Time.

Entries are numbered 1, 2, 3 and so on in the order we count them, with no gaps. Entries our spam filter holds are counted unless we strike them as not a person before the count is fixed; a held entry takes the next number when it is counted. Entries that do not count are never numbered.

Who can enter

Anyone 21 or older who lives in the United States. Not our own household, nor anyone who works with us at Loxley Forest or lives with them.

The draw

On Monday, December 14, 2026, we draw one entry number. The number of entries is fixed and published here by 11:00 AM Eastern Time that day, before the pulse. The seed is the NIST Randomness Beacon's pulse at 12:00 PM Eastern Time, 17:00:00 UTC, that day: its outputValue, 128 hexadecimal digits, published by the US government at beacon.nist.gov, which nobody can pick. If the beacon publishes no pulse for that minute, we use the first pulse it publishes after it.

The winning entry number is that outputValue, read as a hexadecimal number, divided by the number of entries, remainder plus one. We publish the pulse's value and index, the number of entries and the winning number on this page, so anyone can check the draw.

The winner hears from us by email, from mic@loxleyforest.com and from nowhere else, and we never ask for money. They have 72 hours to claim the weekend. If they do not, the next entry number up wins, wrapping around to 1 after the last, and so on for up to three alternates.

Your details

We use your first name and email to run the giveaway and to reach the winner, and for nothing else, unless you tick the letters box. Our privacy page says how we keep them.
