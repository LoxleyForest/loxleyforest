The color

A cathedral of gold and crimson

We have seven seasons, and every one is dramatic. Color Turn is a cathedral of gold and crimson.

Color at elevation arrives earlier and lasts longer. Loxley Forest sits at 1,470 feet, in the foothills above Dahlonega.

Watch the sun drop behind the ridgeline from your private deck. The forest turns gold, then amber, then blue.

It is the most dramatic season, and the most popular. Book early.

Fire as witness

Exchange rings by the fire

The Fire Circle is a boulder fire pit that looks like it emerged from the earth centuries ago, set at the four compass points. It echoes traditions that have existed for thousands of years. Exchange rings beside it.

Afterward the same fire cooks dinner. A stainless steel grill sits over the Fire Circle, for steak, burgers or whatever you fancy.

Up on the sky deck, the fire table keeps you out there longer.

The crossing

The most dramatic aisle in the Southeast

Cross a hundred-foot rope bridge to say your vows. In October it runs through fall leaves to the treehouse canvas, strung with small warm lights.

The hundred-foot rope bridge isn't a gimmick. It's a threshold. The moment you step onto it, you leave the ground behind. By the time you reach the other side, something in your chest has shifted.

Questions

Everything you're wondering

What does the forest look like in October?

The canopy turns gold, then crimson, then amber. From above, the treehouse canvas shows through a canopy of orange, red and gold.

What if it rains on our day?

Rain in the mountains is its own kind of magic, and your photographer will love it. Every couple receives a weather contingency plan. The canopy provides natural protection.

Can we elope on a weekday?

Absolutely, and we'll love you for it. Weekday ceremonies get the best light and the quietest forest.

What else is there to do in the fall?

In Ellijay, B.J. Reece Orchards for apple picking and legendary doughnuts, then walk across to Reece's Cider Company for hard ciders on tap. In Dahlonega, Gold Rush Days on the square.
