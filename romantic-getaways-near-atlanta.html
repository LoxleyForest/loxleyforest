A romantic getaway near Atlanta

A treehouse sixty feet up, just over an hour from Atlanta

Loxley Forest is a hand-built treehouse sixty feet up in the canopy of 42 acres of hardwood cove forest, in Dahlonega, Georgia, just over an hour north of Atlanta. You reach the door across a hundred-foot rope bridge strung with small warm lights.

What a stay is like

The bridge, the deck, the fire and the stars

The crossing comes first. A thirty-foot rope bridge is the warm-up, then the hundred-foot bridge carries you through the trees to your front door. It sways, by design.

Inside, a queen bed is framed by panoramic windows. Outside, a private deck sits above the forest floor, with a fire pit on the ground below.

In the morning, TreeMail leaves things like fresh pastries in the box where you enter the bridges, around 8:30. Or add the Dawn Table, and breakfast is carried across the bridge to your door, with a late checkout to go with it.

After dark, light a fire in the pit on the forest floor, then watch the stars from the deck. When the leaves drop, the canopy opens over you. In the warmer months, the stars show in pockets between the branches, like the sky through a cathedral window.

By day, walk the trails across 42 acres. The only schedule is sunrise and sunset. There are no televisions. On purpose.

The seasons

Fall color. Winter fires.

In fall, the canopy turns gold, then crimson, then amber. The color comes in, the air turns, and the treehouse doesn't look at it. It sits inside it.

In winter, there is frost on the rope bridge, and the bare canopy shows views the leaves keep secret. The treehouse is heated for the cold seasons, and the fire pit feels essential.

In spring, mountain laurel blooms along the trails. In summer, the windows open to the forest and the evenings on the deck run long.

Getting here

Just over an hour north of Atlanta

We're in the foothills above Dahlonega, just over an hour north of Atlanta. The last mile is a mountain road. That's how you know you're close. Detailed directions come with your booking confirmation.

Dahlonega sits at the heart of Georgia's wine country. For dinner, our default is Spirits Tavern, on the square. Call ahead on weekends.

Anniversaries and proposals

An anniversary, a proposal, a wedding in the trees

Couples come to Loxley Forest to celebrate anniversaries, proposals and elopements. When you book, tell us what you're celebrating.

Planning to propose? Ask on the rope bridge, by the Compass Fire Pit or on the canopy deck, and we'll help set the scene. See where to propose in North Georgia, or go straight to proposals at Loxley Forest.

If you'd rather marry here, the hundred-foot rope bridge becomes the aisle. Then stay the night. See elopements.

Questions

Before you come up

How far is Loxley Forest from Atlanta?

Just over an hour north of Atlanta, in the foothills above Dahlonega, Georgia. Detailed directions come with your booking confirmation.

How many guests can stay?

The treehouse sleeps 4 comfortably, and up to 6 guests in all. Children are welcome.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes, up to two well-behaved dogs, with no fee (dogs only, please). The bridge sways sixty feet up, so if you're not sure how your dog will take it, write to us before you book.

Is there WiFi?

Yes. But we encourage you not to use it.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available, so just ask.
