You bring the sauce, the cheese and your favorite toppings. Have them out and ready before the oven gets hot, because once it's going, it goes fast.
This is the sauce-first approach

The method

It sounds weird. It works. Sauce goes in alone for a short first cook, then the cheese and toppings go on for the finish.

Leopard spots and bubbling cheese. That's the one.
Step 1

Preheat

Push in the dial and turn it to light the Roccbox, then turn it up to HIGH. Walk away for 15 minutes.

Step 2

Start with the mini dough

While the oven heats, prep your small dough exactly like the steps below. This is your test run. If it comes out perfect, you just earned an appetizer. If it burns, you just learned exactly how fast this thing cooks, and you saved your real pizza. Either way, you win.

Step 3

Roll out your dough

Lift a ball from the proofer onto your parchment. Press it flat from the middle out with your fingertips, then roll it out into a round. Keep it a size you can slide off the peel and turn easily. Bigger isn't better in a hot little oven.

Step 4

Sauce only, first cook

  • Spread a light coat of sauce on the dough. No cheese. No toppings. Just sauce.
  • Do the shimmy test: slide the dough back and forth on the peel. If it sticks, lift the edge and work it loose before you launch. A dough that won't slide on the peel won't slide off it.
  • Launch it into the oven.
  • Cook for about 1 minute 30 seconds. Watch for the crust to rise and color at the back.
Step 5

Pull it out and dress it

  • Add cheese. Less than you think, about half what your instincts say.
  • Add your toppings.
  • Turn the flame down to HALF. This prevents burnt toppings and raw bottoms.
Step 6

Back in, rotate as you go

  • Slide the dressed pizza back into the oven.
  • Wait for the crust to rise and color before your first turn.
  • Every 20 to 30 seconds, use the small turning peel to rotate it.
  • Total time: no more than 2 minutes.
  • You're watching for leopard spots on the crust and bubbly, golden cheese. When you see it, trust your eyes.
Step 7

Pull and repeat

  • Remove it with the large peel.
  • Turn the oven back to HIGH for the next pizza. That keeps the stone hot.
Step 8

Slice. Eat. Enjoy the view.

Sixty feet up, with the forest going quiet around you and the fire table glowing. This is the whole point.

Worth remembering

Things to remember

  • The mini dough is your tuitionUse it first.
  • Don't rotate too earlyWait for color and rise at the back.
  • Half the cheese you thinkIt's the most common way to sink a pizza.
  • About 3½ to 5 minutesFrom launch to eating, with this method.
  • Any shape you likeNobody said it has to be round. A heart works.
The oven is hot all over. The outside of the Roccbox and the stone stay hot long after the flame is out. Keep little hands back, use the peels, and never leave it burning unattended.

When you're done

Shutting down

Push in the dial and turn it back to off. Let the oven cool on its own with the peels set aside, and leave the stone where it is. A wipe of the counter, and you're back to the view.
