11:00 am

Raven Cliff Falls

The best creek hike in reach. Dodd Creek runs beside you the whole way, spilling over cascade after cascade, until the trail ends at a waterfall pouring straight through a split in a cliff face. Worth every step.

~35 to 40 min drive~5 mi round tripModerate$5 parkingToday onlyDirections
Why today: there's an early stream crossing with no bridge, just logs or a calf-deep wade. Once the rain arrives, it isn't safe until the water drops. Friday is your window.

Your home base

The treehouse

Everything you need to know about the place you'll keep coming back to all weekend.

The bridge

A hundred feet of suspended rope bridge over the holler, with the creek running below. It sways, and it's built to. Cross it again after dark with the lights on. It's a completely different walk at night.

The sky deck

Home to the pizza oven and the projector. Pizza is a Friday plan this weekend, while it's dry.

The fire

The fire pit is at ground level in the trees. Build it the honest way, please: no gas and no accelerants.

The forest kitchen

French press, electric kettle, cast iron, and a Yeti cooler on the deck with the ice taken care of.

Staying connected

Cell service is thin up here, which is part of the point. Wi-Fi runs from the hotspot on the deck.

Monday morning

Your Dawn Table crosses the bridge to your door, and the late checkout means you can linger as long as you like.
