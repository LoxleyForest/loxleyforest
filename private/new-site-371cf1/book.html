Your stay

A day in the canopy

  1. 3:00 PM

    Turn off the paved road onto a gravel path through the forest. The world gets quieter with every turn.

  2. 3:30 PM

    Park at the trailhead. Walk four hundred feet through mountain laurel and fern. Hear the creek before you see it.

  3. 3:45 PM

    Cross the rope bridge for the first time. Your heart beats faster. Then the treehouse door opens.

  4. 4:00 PM

    Unpack. Step onto the deck. Realize you're sixty feet in the air and there isn't a single sound except wind through leaves.

  5. 5:30 PM

    Fire up the pizza oven on the sky deck. Open the bottle you brought. Burn the first pizza. Laugh about it. Nail the second one.

  6. 7:00 PM

    Watch the sun drop behind the ridgeline from your private deck. The forest turns gold, then amber, then blue.

  7. 8:30 PM

    Build a fire. Roast marshmallows. Look up. The Milky Way is right there.

  8. 10:00 PM

    Fall asleep to absolute silence, sixty feet above the forest floor.

  9. 8:30 AM

    Wake up to birdsong. Check the TreeMail box at the entrance to your bridge. Something is waiting inside.

That's just one version. Every stay writes its own story.
