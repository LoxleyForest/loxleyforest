Treehouse design · one oak, two posts

The Johnson Treehouse

A 10 by 10 foot deck 6 feet up at the oak, hung on the tree with Nelson Treehouse hardware and carried on two posts where the yard falls away behind it, under an open gable roof with skylights. Climb in on the right by the cargo net or the steps, up the rock wall on the left, and slide down off the front. Drag to spin it, pinch or scroll to zoom.

What you're looking at

  1. 1

    Nelson Dynamic Yoke on two Tree Fort TABs. A laminated 2x6 triangle hangs on two bolts in the oak, one above the other. Each main beam rests on a nylon plate at a top corner, so the tree can sway and grow while the posts stay put.

  2. 2

    Two main beams, front to back. 3-ply 2x12s, 5 ft 8 in apart, run from the yoke to the back posts. The 2x8 joists sit on top and run side to side.

  3. 3

    Two 6x6 back posts on 24 in footings, on the downhill side. Knee braces run toward the oak and to a doubled 2x8 girt across both posts. There's room for a bench and puck lights underneath.

  4. 4

    Cargo net, right side. 4 ft wide at 50°, with 8 in mesh, a captain's wheel and a bell at the top.

  5. 5

    Rock wall, left side. One 4 by 8 sheet of plywood at 65° with bolt-on holds and a knotted rope fixed at both ends.

  6. 6

    Slide, off the front. It goes out beside the oak and lands toward the house, with a bar over the opening so kids sit first.

  7. 7

    Gable roof with skylights. Metal roofing, open at both ends, with one clear panel in each slope. The front 3 ft of deck stays open under the oak, and the roof stops 3 ft 8 in from the bark.

  8. 8

    Hog-panel rails built like Loxley's. Stained 4x4 posts, a bottom rail 2 in off the deck, a 4 in wire grid, ½ in hardware cloth on the inside so it can't be climbed, and a flat 2x6 cap at 36 in.

  9. 9

    Steps, right side near the front. The easy way up and down for little ones and grown-ups, with handrails on both sides.

Built in for the kids

  • No gaps that trap. The bottom rail sits 2 in off the deck, the wire grid stays under 4 in, and the net mesh is 8 in or more.
  • Rails that can't be climbed. Hardware cloth lines the inside, and the cap is at 36 in.
  • An easy way down. The net and the wall are the fun ways up, and the steps are always there.
  • Soft landings. 12 in of playground chips under everything and 6 ft out, including the slide's landing.
  • No loose ropes. The wall rope is fixed at both ends and the flag pole has no rope. Cap every bolt end, including the lower tree bolt.
