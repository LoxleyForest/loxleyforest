Privacy

Your Privacy at Loxley

Simple, plain-spoken, and personal.

We keep this simple because we believe it should be.

When you book a stay, submit an inquiry, or join our email list, we collect the information you provide — your name, email, phone number, and stay preferences. That's it.

We use this information for one purpose: to communicate with you about your stay, your experience, and occasional updates from the forest. We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Period.

If you receive emails from us and decide you'd rather not, every email includes an unsubscribe link that works immediately. No hoops.

We use cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website so we can make it better. No personal data is sold through these tools.

If you have questions about your privacy or want us to remove your information entirely, email stay@loxleyforest.com or call 706-502-4823. We'll take care of it personally.

— Mick & Livy
