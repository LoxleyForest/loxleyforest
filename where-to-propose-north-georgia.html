Where to propose in North Georgia

On a rope bridge, sixty feet up

For a proposal in North Georgia, Loxley Forest has a hundred-foot rope bridge strung with small warm lights, sixty feet up in a hardwood cove forest in Dahlonega, just over an hour north of Atlanta. Ask on the bridge, by the fire or on the treehouse deck, then stay the night.

How planning works

We keep the secret. You keep the words.

Tell us the date you have in mind and how you picture it: golden hour on the bridge, or a fire after dark. Then we plan it with you.

  • We keep it a secret. We plan it with you and no one else. To your partner it can be a night away in the trees, right up until you ask.
  • We coordinate a photographer. One you choose, or one we suggest, so they know where to be and when.
  • We set the scene. Things like flowers, lanterns and champagne on ice, ready where you plan to ask.

After the yes

Stay the night in the trees

The treehouse is yours until morning: a queen bed framed by panoramic windows, a private deck above the forest floor, and the forest all around you.

In the morning, add the Dawn Table: breakfast carried across the bridge to your door, with a late checkout to go with it.

Questions

Before you ask

Can you keep the proposal a secret?

Yes. We plan it with you and no one else, and to your partner it can be a night away in the trees right up until you ask.

Can we have a photographer there?

Yes. We coordinate with a photographer you choose, or one we suggest, so they know where to be and when.

Where on the property can I propose?

On the hundred-foot rope bridge, by the Compass Fire Pit on the forest floor, or on the private deck of the treehouse, sixty feet up.

How far is it from Atlanta?

Just over an hour north of Atlanta, in the foothills above Dahlonega, Georgia. Detailed directions come with your booking confirmation.

Can we stay the night after?

Yes. Book your dates and the treehouse is yours until morning, with check-in from 3:00 PM and check-out by 11:00 AM.
