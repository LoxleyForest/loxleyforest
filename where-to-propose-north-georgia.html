At sunrise

Preacher's Rock from Woody Gap

A short climb with a massive reward: one of the best views in North Georgia, on the Appalachian Trail. It faces southeast, so at sunrise you're above the clouds.

Leave the treehouse by 6:30 AM. The best effort-to-reward ratio in the area.

At sunset

Kaya Vineyard an hour before sunset

270-degree mountain views from a hilltop, the most dramatic vista of any winery in the area. Get there an hour before sunset with a glass in hand. The best free sunset show in North Georgia.

By water

A lake loop, and two waterfalls

Lake Zwerner is an easy, scenic loop around the reservoir. Flat, beautiful, accessible. Coffee at Jethro's first, then the loop. The perfect easy morning.

Amicalola Falls is the waterfall to see. The East Ridge Loop gives you the best of it. Stop at B.J. Reece Orchards on the way back for cider and doughnuts.

Raven Cliff Falls is for guests who actually want to hike: a stunning waterfall, stream crossings, beautiful forest, real hiking.

And one you can stay at

Ask the question sixty feet up

Halfway across the bridge, you stop noticing the height and start noticing the silence. That is a good place to ask.

Ask on the bridge at golden hour, by the fire after dark, or on the deck with nothing around you but trees. Tell us which, and we'll help you get the moment right.

Then stay the night. Wake up engaged in the trees.
