Wine country

15 wineries. 20 minutes. Start here.

Dahlonega sits at the heart of Georgia's wine country. These are the three we send every guest to, and two more for the ones who want to keep going.

  • Wolf Mountain Vineyards

    About 15 min

    The crown jewel. Award-winning wines, hilltop views, and a Sunday brunch that books up fast. If you can only do one winery, this is it.

    Mick's pick Call ahead for brunch. It fills up.

  • Montaluce Winery & Restaurant

    About 20 min

    Best food in wine country. Floor-to-ceiling windows over the vineyard. Made for a long lunch with wine.

    Mick's pick The restaurant is the real draw.

  • Kaya Vineyard & Winery

    About 12 min

    270-degree mountain views from a hilltop, the most dramatic vista of any winery in the area. Get there an hour before sunset with a glass in hand.

    Mick's pick The best free sunset show in North Georgia.

  • Doghobble Wine Farm

    About 5 min

    A working farm with wine. Dogs, chickens, alpacas. The most authentic, unpretentious winery in the area, and great food too.

    Mick's pick For guests who want real, not polished.

  • Cavender Creek Vineyards

    About 5 min

    Family-owned, laid-back, estate-grown wines. Right around the corner from Doghobble.

    Mick's pick Pair it with Doghobble for a slow afternoon close to the treehouse.

Hiking and waterfalls

Waterfalls, summits and the Appalachian Trail

Some of the most dramatic hiking in the Southeast, all within 45 minutes. From easy waterfall walks to above-the-clouds sunrise hikes.

  • Amicalola Falls

    About 45 min

    The tallest cascading waterfall in Georgia, 729 feet, with several viewing platforms. The East Ridge Loop gives you the best of it.

    Mick's pick The waterfall to see. Stop at B.J. Reece Orchards on the way back for cider and doughnuts.

  • Preacher's Rock from Woody Gap

    About 25 min

    A short climb with a massive reward: one of the best views in North Georgia, 2 miles round trip on the Appalachian Trail. It faces southeast, so at sunrise you're above the clouds.

    Mick's pick Leave the treehouse by 6:30 AM. The best effort-to-reward ratio in the area.

  • Raven Cliff Falls

    About 35 min

    4.9 miles round trip to a stunning waterfall. Stream crossings, beautiful forest, real hiking.

    Mick's pick For guests who actually want to hike. Stop in Helen after for the mountain coaster.

  • Lake Zwerner

    About 7 min

    An easy, scenic loop around the reservoir. Flat, beautiful, accessible.

    Mick's pick Coffee at Jethro's first, then the loop. The perfect easy morning.

Downtown Dahlonega

Gold rush history and small-town character

America's first gold rush started here in 1828. The square still has its courthouse with the gold-leaf steeple, surrounded by local shops, restaurants and places that have nothing to do with a chain.

  • Spirits Tavern

    Elevated American fare on the square. Good cocktails, a solid menu. Our default dinner recommendation.

    Mick's pick Call ahead on weekends.

  • Picnic Cafe

    The mayor's wife bakes. Small-town, personal, genuine. Great sandwiches and baked goods.

    Mick's pick The perfect quick lunch before you explore the square.

  • Foothill Grill

    About 10 min

    A breakfast legend. The cinnamon rolls are what people talk about.

    Mick's pick The cinnamon rolls. Get there early on weekends.

  • Consolidated Gold Mine

    About 8 min

    200 feet underground, a constant 60 degrees, a 40-minute guided tour through the original tunnels. Gold panning included.

    Mick's pick Our number one rainy-day pick. Underground means weatherproof.

  • Jethro's Coffee House

    The coffee stop. Good coffee, a cozy room, right on the square.

    Mick's pick Coffee at Jethro's before a walk at Lake Zwerner. That's the move.

  • Dahlonega Fudge Factory

    Family-owned since 1982. More than 15 kinds of fudge, and the Dahlonega Nuggets: pecans in caramel, dipped in chocolate. An institution.

    Mick's pick Everyone stops here. It's part of the trip.

  • Paul Thomas Chocolates

    More than 120 handmade recipes, made on site. The elevated chocolate option.

    Mick's pick For couples who want something special to bring back to the treehouse.

Day trips

Three mountain towns within an hour

When you want to explore beyond Dahlonega, these three towns extend the adventure.

  • Helen, GA

    About 35 min

    A Bavarian village in the mountains. The real draw is the Alpine Coaster, gravity-powered with you in control of the speed, and $5 unlimited river tubing in summer.

    Mick's pick Do the coaster, walk the village, get ice cream. A full afternoon.

  • Ellijay, GA

    About 45 min

    Georgia's apple capital. In fall, B.J. Reece Orchards for apple picking and legendary doughnuts, then walk across to Reece's Cider Company for 16 hard ciders on tap.

    Mick's pick In fall, this is the day trip.

  • Blue Ridge, GA

    About 1 hr

    A vintage scenic railway along the Toccoa River. Zip lines and aerial courses at the Adventure Park. A more polished downtown with shops and galleries.

    Mick's pick Book the railway ahead. It sells out.
